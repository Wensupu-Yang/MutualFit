# MutualFit MVP design

**Intent:** Hackathon prototype showing that providers who look identical by rating
rank differently once customer needs and provider preferences are known.
Built in about 75 minutes; synthetic data only.

**Success:** In one short demo, a customer's words change the ranking, every
change is explained, and moving a provider's points re-ranks live.

**Non-goals:** accounts, booking, database, provider chat, synthetic reviews, deployment.
**Anti-goals:** AI inventing scores; a demo that dies without network; saved
responses passed off as live AI; claims that truthful disclosure is proven.

## Approaches
1. **AI reads, code ranks (picked by user).** Fast, traceable; nuance outside categories won't affect rank.
2. AI does everything: more nuance, but slower, variable, undermines the points budget.
3. Scripted: safest, but not real AI.

Reason for 1: live sliders and traceable explanations are the core of the demo.

## Decided by user
Barbershop and plumbing; limited preference budget; optional disclosure;
Nebius Token Factory via OpenAI client, `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-fast`,
base URL `https://api.tokenfactory.eu-south1.nebius.com/v1/`, `NEBIUS_API_KEY` in `.env`.

## Builder's calls
**Stack:** FastAPI + one plain HTML/JS page, no frontend build. Key stays server-side.

**Categories are concrete.** "Specialisation" cannot distinguish curls from fades,
so points go on specific items, about 8 per industry:
- Barber: fades/classic, beard, long styles, curly/textured, transformations,
  treatments, follows your brief, creative freedom, unhurried appointments.
- Plumbing: small repairs, maintenance, installations, renovations, complex
  faults, urgent jobs, fixed-price work, diagnostic work.
Price is a fact checked against the customer's budget, not a points category.

**Providers:** four per industry, all 4.9 stars. A, B, C from the brief, plus D,
which has many reviews and has not disclosed preferences.

**Ranking (browser code, pure function):**
- Needs: weights summing to 1. Provider: 10 points, whole numbers.
- `base = rating * 10 + log10(reviews)` (the conventional ranking)
- `customerFit = sum(need_c * min(1, points_c / 4))`
- `providerFit = points on needed categories / 10`
- `mutual = sqrt(customerFit * providerFit)`
- `score = base + 30 * mutual`, bonus halved if over budget
- Undisclosed: bonus 0. Disclosure never lowers a provider's own score, but others may overtake.

**Explanations:** generated by template from these numbers: what matches, the
trade-off, and the provider's side ("0 points on complex faults"). Undisclosed: "fit unknown".

**AI:** receives the chosen industry's categories and the conversation; returns JSON:
need weights, budget, summary, one optional follow-up (at most two in total).
Server drops unknown categories and normalises weights. Customer can edit the needs.
Invalid response, 8s timeout or no key: use a saved example for the sample
prompts, labelled "saved example"; for other text, show an error and manual sliders.

## Screen
Industry tabs; customer text and editable needs at left; the two rankings side by
side; provider point sliders below with points remaining. Footer: synthetic data,
incentive effect untested.

## Stages (each ends in a working demo)
1. 30 min: barbershop, AI extraction + saved examples, two rankings, reasons.
2. 15 min: provider sliders, disclose toggle, live re-rank.
3. 15 min: adaptive follow-up questions.
4. 15 min: plumbing data.
Optional: provider description to suggested points.

**Tests:** ranking only: expected winner in both brief scenarios, undisclosed bonus
is 0, disclosure never lowers own score, over-budget halves bonus.

## Revision 1 (user, after first demo)
- Ratings vary from 4.8 to 5.0 instead of a flat 4.9 (supersedes "all 4.9 stars").
- Six point categories per industry (asked for 5-7); budget stays 10 points.
- Client and provider views are separate tabs; provider view is simple steppers.
- Client view compares the two rankings side by side with movement lines.
- Builder's call: provider view shows "rank for the current customer, sharing vs
  not sharing", because the rankings are no longer visible on that tab.
